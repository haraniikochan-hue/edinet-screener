## Key Question 1: Indeed Hiring Lab — US Job Postings Index, new postings, sectors, wages, October commentary

### Takeaway
Indeed's US Job Postings Index was 103.8 in mid/late October 2026 (3.8% above the Feb-2020 baseline), up 0.3% m/m and +2.8% y/y (vs. −8.1% y/y in October 2025), with breadth improving (68% of occupational sectors above year-ago), but Hiring Lab explicitly says it is "premature to say the labor market is strengthening"; the new-postings index (99.2) is drifting sideways-to-slightly-down and posted wage growth has cooled to 2.4% y/y, below 3.7% CPI inflation.

### Cited Findings
- **Job Postings Index (JPI): 103.8 as of October 16, 2026**, "almost 4% above" the Feb 1, 2020 pre-pandemic baseline of 100. Monthly change **+0.3% m/m**; **+2.8% y/y**, versus **−8.1% y/y in October 2025**. (Reference: Oct 16, 2026; published Oct 22, 2026) — [Indeed Hiring Lab, US Labor Market Snapshot October 2026](https://hiringlab.indeed.com/2026/10/22/us-labor-market-snapshot-october-2026/)

- **New postings index: 99.2**, "drifting sideways, with a slightly downward trend even after smoothing." (Reference: Oct 2026; published Oct 22, 2026) — [Indeed Hiring Lab](https://hiringlab.indeed.com/2026/10/22/us-labor-market-snapshot-october-2026/)

- Sector pattern (healthcare/engineering/manufacturing strong; software, HR weak) is consistent with Revelio's October sector detail (health care +17.7k, Information −4.1k, PBS −3.4k) — see Q3.

## Key Question 2: LinkUp — job listings and nonfarm payroll forecast for October 2026

### Takeaway
LinkUp (via Toby Dayton's "Diggings" newsletter, Nov 4, 2026) forecasts **+180,000** October nonfarm payrolls — nearly 3x the +65,000 consensus — based on September posting data, even as its own October listings fell (new openings −10.5%, total −3.3%).

### Cited Findings
- "we're forecasting a net gain of 180,000 jobs in October, well above the consensus estimate of 65,000 jobs." Forecast uses **September** job-posting data because postings lead filled jobs. (Reference: Oct 2026 forecast; published Nov 4, 2026) — [Diggings on the Job Market (LinkUp CEO Toby Dayton)](https://diggings.substack.com/p/linkup-forecasting-a-net-gain-of-499)
- LinkUp listings labeled "October": **new job openings −10.5%; total job openings −3.3%**. Labor demand rose in manufacturing and fell in services; declines spanned both blue-collar and white-collar roles; new and total openings fell in most of the country. Year-to-date LinkUp data "shows a stronger labor demand picture than JOLTS." (Reference: Oct 2026; published Nov 4, 2026) — [Diggings](https://diggings.substack.com/p/linkup-forecasting-a-net-gain-of-499)
- Track-record context: Richmond Fed found LinkUp has the **highest sign accuracy (93.8% of months)** but **lowest correlation with BLS (0.530)** and the largest volatility relative to BLS (mean ratio to BLS 1.448, SD 1.972) among ADP/Revelio/LinkUp, Jan 2022–2025. (Published March 2026) — [Richmond Fed Economic Brief 26-08](https://www.richmondfed.org/publications/research/economic_brief/2026/eb_26-08)

### Inferences
- LinkUp's model has a documented tendency to overshoot BLS magnitudes (mean ratio 1.45x); its +180k should be read as "direction: positive/above consensus," not as a point estimate.
- The contradiction between the +180k forecast and the −10.5% drop in October new listings suggests November payrolls (not October) are at risk if the listings decline persists.

### Gaps
- Could not access LinkUp's own site/insights page; the Substack is the only source for the forecast and gives no levels for new/total/removed listings (charts only). No removed-listings figure found.

### Cited Findings
- Headline: "the economy added **26k jobs in October 2026**, marking the third consecutive month of job gains after mostly negative readings earlier in the year." (Reference: Oct 2026; published Nov 5, 2026) — [Revelio Labs RPLS October 2026](https://www.reveliolabs.com/news/rpls/rpls-us-jobs-report-the-us-economy-adds-26k-jobs-in-october); [PR Newswire release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/revelio-public-labor-statistics-reports-us-economy-adds-26k-jobs-in-october-302930790.html)
